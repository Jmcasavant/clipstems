FFmpeg
------
The "ffmpeg" folder holds an unmodified build of FFmpeg (ffmpeg.exe,
ffprobe.exe and their libraries), licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public
License, version 3 or later. Its license text is ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt.

Clipstems runs ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe as separate programs; it does not
link to FFmpeg's libraries. You may replace the folder's contents with another
build of FFmpeg, or point Clipstems at one with the CLIPSTEMS_FFMPEG
environment variable.

The build, its version, and links to its exact source code and build scripts
are listed in ffmpeg\ffmpeg.json:
  - FFmpeg source:   https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
  - Build scripts:   https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.
